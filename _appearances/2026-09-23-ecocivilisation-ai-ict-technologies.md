I'll be co-moderating **AI and ICT Technologies Challenged** with Ekaterina Matveeva, a mini-conference in Ecocivilisation Earth's year-long AI Challenged series.

## Event Details

**Date:** Wednesday, September 23, 2026  
**Time:** 14:00 – 18:00 CET  
**Format:** Virtual, Zoom  
**Series:** AI Challenged (2026)

## About This Event

AI Challenged is a year of sessions on how AI intersects with humanity, culture, geopolitics, education, regulation, media, art, ICT, medicine, and infrastructure. The series is virtual and aimed at raising awareness of AI's limits and risks, identifying regulatory and research needs, and feeding into a book capturing how AI is perceived at this moment.
