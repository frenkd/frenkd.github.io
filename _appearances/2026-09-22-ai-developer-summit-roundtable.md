I'll be moderating the roundtable at the AI Developer Summit, an evening hosted by Astra AI and Vercel for people building with AI.

## Event Details

**Date:** Tuesday, September 22, 2026  
**Time:** From 17:00  
**Location:** Odiseja, Šmartinska cesta 152, Ljubljana  
**Entry:** Free, registration required

## About This Event

The programme includes technical talks from Vercel and Astra AI, followed by a panel and audience Q&A, **The era of 100× engineers**: how AI changes the work, the team, and what's possible.

Announced speakers include Guillermo Rauch, Lars Grammel, and Boris Besemer (Vercel), and Tjaž Silovšek (Astra AI).
